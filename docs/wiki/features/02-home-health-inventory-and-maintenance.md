# Home Health, Inventory & Maintenance

This is the **understand-the-home** cluster: it's where C2C builds the context — what's in the house (rooms, appliances, systems), its upkeep state (maintenance tasks, seasonal checklists, habits), its condition (inspections, room health, energy), and its paperwork (documents, records, the Seller's Vault) — that every other cluster in this wiki reads from rather than asking the homeowner to re-enter. The strategic point of this data isn't record-keeping for its own sake: an item logged here is what lets Coverage flag a gap, Guidance diagnose a broken appliance without re-asking its age, or Sale Readiness compute real progress instead of a self-reported checklist. Most of these tools are property-scoped — nearly every route below hangs off `/api/properties/:propertyId/...` and is gated by `propertyAuthMiddleware`. Four Gemini-powered AI tools sit inside this cluster (Appliance Oracle, Visual Inspector/AI Room Scan, Energy Auditor, Document/Material photo intelligence); the rest — room "insights," maintenance prediction, plant recommendations — are deterministic, rules-based logic despite AI-sounding names. **Correction, from a fresh redirect-table audit of `next.config.js`:** the "which top-level routes are live vs. redirected" picture here was incomplete. `/dashboard/inventory` and `/dashboard/documents` are **not** `JobHubRedirectPage` stubs going straight to a property-scoped page as previously stated — they're config-level redirects (`next.config.js`, "Vault consolidation") to `/dashboard/vault?tab=assets` / `?tab=documents`, which is itself a `JobHubRedirectPage` to `/dashboard/properties/:id/vault` — which **redirects again**, this time to that property's Inventory page with a tab param. `/dashboard/seasonal` is also now redirected (`/dashboard/maintenance?filter=seasonal`), so it's not a "still live top-level page" as this line previously said — see the Maintenance section below. `oracle`, `visual-inspector`, `energy`, `inspection-report`, `home-lab`, `daily-snapshot` were not found in the redirect table and are presumably still live as described, but weren't independently re-verified in this pass.

### Appliance & Item Inventory
- **What it does:** Lets a homeowner catalog rooms and the items/appliances in each one — brand, model, serial number, purchase/install dates, cost, warranty and insurance links — either by hand, via barcode/UPC lookup, XLSX bulk import, OCR of a nameplate photo, or an AI room-scan from a batch of photos.
- **User flow:**
  1. Homeowner opens Inventory for a property, sees rooms (`/dashboard/properties/[id]/inventory` → `InventoryClient.tsx`, room list at `/inventory/rooms`).
  2. Creates a room, then adds items manually, or taps "Scan room" to upload up to ~10 photos for AI extraction, or scans a barcode, or photographs an appliance label for OCR, or bulk-imports an XLSX.
  3. AI/OCR/barcode paths produce **draft** items for review (accept/edit/dismiss, individually or in bulk) before they become real `InventoryItem` rows.
  4. Item detail page (`/inventory/items/[itemId]`) supports linking documents/warranties/insurance, and running coverage-gap / replace-vs-repair analysis per item.
  5. Export the whole inventory to CSV; view/rollback a past bulk-import batch.
- **Frontend:** `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/properties/[id]/inventory/InventoryClient.tsx`, `.../inventory/rooms/RoomsClient.tsx`, `.../inventory/items/[itemId]`, `.../inventory/coverage/CoverageClient.tsx`; API wrapper `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/inventory/inventoryApi.ts`; `components/manage-rooms/*` (AddRoomForm, RoomsList, RoomRow); `app/(dashboard)/dashboard/components/inventory/RoomScanModal.tsx`. **Correction:** old top-level `/dashboard/inventory` redirects to `/dashboard/vault?tab=assets` (`next.config.js`, not a direct `JobHubRedirectPage` to a property-scoped inventory page as previously stated) — see the intro paragraph above for the full chain.
- **Backend:** `apps/backend/src/routes/inventory.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`, e.g. `GET/POST /properties/:propertyId/inventory/rooms`, `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /properties/:propertyId/inventory/items(/:itemId)`, `POST .../inventory/barcode/lookup`, `POST .../inventory/ocr/label`, `POST .../inventory/rooms/:roomId/scan-ai`, `GET .../inventory/rooms/:roomId/scan-ai/:sessionId`, draft CRUD (`.../inventory/drafts`, `/:draftId/confirm|dismiss`, `/bulk-confirm|bulk-dismiss`), `GET .../inventory/export` (CSV), `GET .../inventory/import/template` + `POST .../inventory/import` (XLSX) + `.../import-batches(/:batchId/rollback)`, `GET .../coverage-gaps`, `GET/POST .../items/:itemId/coverage-analysis` and `/replace-repair`. Controllers: `inventory.controller.ts`, `inventoryRoomScan.controller.ts`, `inventoryOcr.controller.ts`, `inventoryDraft.controller.ts`, `inventoryImport.controller.ts`, `coverageAnalysis.controller.ts`, `replaceRepairAnalysis.controller.ts`. Services: `inventory.service.ts` (barcode lookup provider), `inventoryOcr.service.ts`, `inventoryImport.service.ts`, `services/roomScan/roomScan.service.ts` + `provider.ts`.
- **Data:** `InventoryRoom`, `InventoryItem`, `InventoryImportBatch`, inventory draft model(s), plus links to `Warranty`, `InsurancePolicy`, `Document`.
- **Notes:** AI room-scan (`scan-ai`) uses **Gemini** (`services/roomScan/provider.ts`, model default `gemini-2.0-flash`, configurable via `ROOM_SCAN_GEMINI_MODEL`) to turn photos into draft items. Label OCR (`ocr/label`) is **not** Gemini — it uses local **Tesseract.js** (`inventoryOcr.service.ts`), with an `INVENTORY_OCR_DEBUG_ENABLED` debug mode gated by an email allowlist. OCR is nominally behind `requirePremiumForOcr` but that middleware is currently commented out in the route (`//requirePremiumForOcr`) — effectively unmetered right now. Barcode lookup normalizes UPCs and calls an external product-lookup provider via `InventoryService.lookupBarcode`.

### Maintenance Tasks & Seasonal Checklists (dual-system note)
- **What it does:** Tracks recurring and one-off upkeep work — filter changes, gutter cleaning, HVAC service — surfaced either from a seasonal/climate-aware checklist, rule-predicted maintenance forecasts (**not** AI — see the deterministic `MAINTENANCE_RULES` table noted below; a prior version of this line called these "AI-predicted," contradicting this section's own Notes), the Action Center, or entered by hand. Completing or scheduling a task can link it to a provider booking.
- **There are two task systems in this codebase, one of them deprecated:**
  - **Legacy `ChecklistItem`** (`checklist.routes.ts` → `checklist.controller.ts` → `ChecklistService`, mounted at `/api/checklist`) is explicitly marked `@deprecated` in the controller header, sends `X-API-Deprecated`/`X-API-Sunset-Date` response headers, and points callers at two replacements: `/api/home-buyer-tasks/*` for pre-purchase journeys (covered in the onboarding/buyer-flow wiki page) and `/api/maintenance-tasks/*` for ownership-phase care (this page). There is no corresponding top-level `/dashboard/checklist` page — that route directory exists in the frontend tree but is empty, confirming the UI has already moved off it.
  - **`PropertyMaintenanceTask`** (`propertyMaintenanceTask.routes.ts` → `propertyMaintenanceTask.controller.ts` → `PropertyMaintenanceTaskService`, mounted at `/api/maintenance-tasks`) is the live system backing the Maintenance page today. Tasks can originate from four `source`s: `USER_CREATED`, `from-action-center` (idempotent, keyed off Action Center recommendations), `from-seasonal/:seasonalItemId` (promoted from a seasonal checklist item), and `from-templates`.
  - **Seasonal checklists** (`seasonalChecklist.routes.ts` → `SeasonalChecklistController`) are their own model (climate-zone-aware, per-season item lists) with a legacy `add-to-tasks` endpoint (deprecated, "still works for backward compatibility") and the current `add-to-maintenance` / `remove-from-maintenance` endpoints that create/unlink a `PropertyMaintenanceTask` via `seasonalChecklistItemId`. Removing the link does **not** delete the maintenance task the homeowner may have since edited.
- **User flow:**
  1. Homeowner opens Maintenance (`/dashboard/properties/[id]/maintenance`, which renders the shared `MaintenancePageClient`), sees open vs. completed `PropertyMaintenanceTask`s with priority/status filters.
  2. Creates a task manually, or visits Seasonal (`/dashboard/seasonal` — **correction:** this now redirects to `/dashboard/maintenance?filter=seasonal` per `next.config.js`, rather than being a separate live page) to see season-appropriate recommendations by climate zone, and adds critical ones to maintenance ("Add all critical").
  3. Marks tasks complete (optionally recording `actualCost`), snoozes/dismisses seasonal items, or links a task to a booking once a provider is engaged.
  4. Maintenance Prediction can proactively generate forecasted tasks (e.g., "HVAC filter due") that appear as pending predictions before becoming tasks.
- **Frontend:** `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/maintenance/MaintenancePageClient.tsx` (reused by the property-scoped page), `.../dashboard/seasonal/page.tsx` + `.../seasonal/settings/page.tsx`, `components/seasonal/SeasonalChecklistModal.tsx`, `lib/hooks/useSeasonalChecklists.ts`, `components/tasks/*` (`PropertyMaintenanceList.tsx`, `CreateMaintenanceTaskModal.tsx`, `EditMaintenanceTaskModal.tsx`, `MaintenanceTaskCard.tsx`, `TaskFilterPanel.tsx`, badges). `maintenance-setup/page.tsx` handles initial template-based task setup. `HomeBuyerChecklist.tsx` in `components/tasks/` is the separate purchase-journey checklist UI.
- **Backend:** `propertyMaintenanceTask.routes.ts`: `GET /property/:propertyId(/stats)`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /:taskId`, `POST /property/:propertyId`, `POST /from-action-center`, `POST /from-seasonal/:seasonalItemId`, `POST /from-templates`, `PATCH /:taskId/status`, `POST /:taskId/link-booking`. `seasonalChecklist.routes.ts`: `GET/PUT /properties/:propertyId/climate`, `GET /properties/:propertyId/seasonal-checklists`, `GET /seasonal-checklists/:checklistId`, `POST /seasonal-checklists/generate`, `POST /seasonal-checklists/:checklistId/dismiss`, `POST .../add-all-critical`, and per-item `add-to-tasks` (deprecated), `add-to-maintenance`, `remove-from-maintenance`, `dismiss`, `restore`, `snooze`. `maintenance.routes.ts` (`/api/maintenance-templates`): `GET /`, `POST /custom-items`. `maintenancePrediction.routes.ts`: `POST /properties/:propertyId/maintenance-predictions/generate`, `GET .../maintenance-predictions`, `PATCH .../maintenance-predictions/:predictionId/status`.
- **Data:** `PropertyMaintenanceTask`, legacy `ChecklistItem`, `SeasonalChecklist`/`SeasonalChecklistItem`, `MaintenancePrediction` (or equivalent forecast model), `PropertyClimateZone`-style settings.
- **Notes:** Maintenance Prediction (`maintenancePrediction.service.ts`) is **rule-based, not AI** — a hardcoded `MAINTENANCE_RULES` table (HVAC/water heater/roof intervals, base priority, seasonal tune-up flag) drives forecast generation; it also calls `incrementStreak` for gamification. This is the exact "two task systems" trap flagged previously: any new feature that needs to read/write maintenance work should target `PropertyMaintenanceTask`, not `ChecklistItem`.

### AI Room Scan, Visual Inspector & Appliance Oracle
- **What it does:** Three related but separately-routed AI features that turn photos or inventory data into homeowner-facing insight: the Appliance Oracle predicts replacement timing/cost for existing inventory items; the Visual Inspector analyzes uploaded property photos per room for condition issues; Room Insights scores each room's health from inventory/coverage completeness (no AI).
- **User flow (Appliance Oracle):** Homeowner opens Oracle (`/dashboard/oracle`, or via Home Lab), selects a property, taps "Generate report"; sees per-appliance replacement-risk predictions with estimated cost/timing, backed by `ApplianceOracle.tsx`.
- **User flow (Visual Inspector):** Homeowner opens Visual Inspector (`/dashboard/visual-inspector`), uploads up to 20 room photos tagged with room type, submits; sees an AI-generated condition report with flagged issues and estimated costs.
- **User flow (Room Insights):** From the property's Rooms hub (`/dashboard/properties/[id]/rooms` → `RoomsHubClient.tsx`), each room shows a health band (Good/Needs Attention/At Risk) with "how to improve" tips, a profile questionnaire, a per-room checklist, and a timeline of tasks/incidents for that room.
- **Frontend:** `components/ApplianceOracle.tsx`, `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/oracle/page.tsx`; `components/VisualInspector.tsx`, `.../visual-inspector/page.tsx`; `.../properties/[id]/rooms/RoomsHubClient.tsx`, `.../rooms/[roomId]/RoomShowcaseClient.tsx`, `components/rooms/MobileRoomsCards.tsx`, `components/rooms/RoomListCard.tsx`. `/dashboard/home-lab` (`HomeLabPage`) is a searchable tool-launcher card grid (not a feature itself) that links into these and other property tools.
- **Backend:** `applianceOracle.routes.ts` mounted at `/api/oracle`: `GET /predict/:propertyId`, `GET /summary` (stubbed — returns a placeholder message, aggregation "not yet implemented"). `visualInspector.routes.ts` mounted at `/api/visual-inspector`: `POST /analyze`. `roomInsights.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/inventory/rooms/:roomId/insights`, `PATCH .../rooms/:roomId` and `/profile`, checklist-item CRUD under `.../checklist-items`, `GET .../timeline`. Services: `applianceOracle.service.ts`, `visualInspector.service.ts`, `roomInsights.service.ts` (+ `roomHealthScore.service.ts`).
- **Data:** `InventoryItem` (Oracle reads existing items), room-scan/inspection result records, `InventoryRoom.profile` (JSON questionnaire), room-scoped checklist items, coverage-gap data (`coverageGap.service.ts`) feeding room health scoring.
- **Notes:** Appliance Oracle and Visual Inspector both call **Gemini** directly (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, both log a warning and degrade rather than hard-fail when the key is unset — Oracle falls back to "basic" recommendations). Both are rate-limited (`aiOracleRateLimiter`, `expensiveAiRateLimiter`). **Room Insights is explicitly not AI** — the route comment says "Insights summary (no AI required)"; it's a deterministic score from item count, docs-linked count, and coverage gaps. The Oracle `/summary` cross-property aggregation endpoint is a stub — worth flagging as "planned / not confirmed live" if referenced elsewhere.

### Inspections (Hub, Legacy Report Analyzer, Predictions)
- **What it does:** Lets a homeowner (or buyer) upload a professional inspection report PDF and get it turned into a structured, actionable list of findings — with different downstream flows depending on whether the property is being bought (negotiation package) or sold (fix/disclose decisions) — plus an older, simpler "upload and get an AI summary" analyzer that still exists in parallel.
- **User flow (Inspection Hub — current):**
  1. Homeowner opens Inspection Hub for a property (`/dashboard/properties/[id]/inspection-hub`), sees an overview of reports and open items.
  2. Uploads a PDF report (up to 200MB); it's parsed into `InspectionFinding`s with severity.
  3. Reviews findings: update, dismiss, "accept as work" (creates a maintenance task or project depending on severity/cost via `resolveFindingWorkPolicy` — a $1,500 threshold routes expensive MAJOR findings to a draft Project instead of a plain task; SAFETY findings route to the Guidance engine), or record a work disposition.
  4. Pre-purchase: generate a negotiation package from findings. Pre-listing: fill out fix/disclose decisions per finding.
  5. Can compare multiple reports over time (`/compare`) and confirm a report as reviewed (write-back preview → confirm).
- **User flow (legacy Inspection Report Analyzer):** `/dashboard/inspection-report` lets a user upload a PDF directly (bypassing the Hub's finding workflow) and get an AI-generated overall score/condition, critical/high-priority issue counts, and a generated maintenance calendar; also fires a guidance-engine signal if issues are significant.
- **Frontend:** Hub: `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/properties/[id]/inspection-hub/page.tsx`, `.../inspection-hub/upload/page.tsx`, `.../inspection-hub/[reportId]/page.tsx`, `.../inspection-hub/open-items/page.tsx`. Legacy analyzer: `.../dashboard/inspection-report/page.tsx` + `components/InspectionReportAnalyzer.tsx`.
- **Backend:** Hub: `inspectionHub.routes.ts` mounted at `/api` — `GET /properties/:propertyId/inspection-hub`, `GET/POST .../reports`, `GET/DELETE .../reports/:reportId`, `GET/PATCH .../reports/:reportId/findings(/:findingId)`, `POST .../findings/:findingId/dismiss|accept-as-work|work-disposition`, `GET .../write-back-preview`, `POST .../confirm`, `GET .../open-items`, `POST /findings/:findingId/resolve`, `GET .../compare`, `POST .../negotiation-package` (PRE_PURCHASE only), `GET/PATCH .../fix-disclose` (PRE_LISTING only). Controller `inspectionHub.controller.ts`, service `inspectionHub.service.ts` (routes findings into the `homeOperations` work-item module — `resolveAndUpsertWorkItem`, `transitionWorkItem`). Legacy: `inspectionReport.routes.ts` mounted at `/api/inspection-reports` — `POST /upload`, `GET /:reportId`, `GET /property/:propertyId`, `GET /:reportId/maintenance-calendar`; service `inspectionAnalysis.service.ts`.
- **Data:** Inspection report + `InspectionFinding` models (severity, cost estimates), links into `homeOperations` `WorkItem`s, `PropertyMaintenanceTask` / `Project` (via accept-as-work), Guidance journeys (`guidanceJourneyService`).
- **Notes:** `inspectionAnalysis.service.ts` (the legacy analyzer) calls **Gemini** (`gemini-2.0-flash`) directly on report text. The Hub's `inspectionHub.service.ts` itself contains no Gemini calls — it's a deterministic policy/workflow layer over findings (parsing likely happens elsewhere in the upload pipeline; not traced further here). The two inspection surfaces (Hub vs. legacy analyzer) are not obviously reconciled — the legacy analyzer's reports don't appear to feed the Hub's `InspectionFinding` model, so a homeowner could plausibly have inspection data siloed in either place depending which page they used. Flagging this as a real duplication, not just a naming quirk.

### Home Status Board, Daily Home Pulse & Home Habit Coach
- **What it does:** Three lightweight "at a glance" surfaces: a Status Board showing per-system/appliance status chips for the whole property; a Daily Snapshot ("pulse") summarizing day-over-day risk/score deltas with quick micro-actions; and a Habit Coach that surfaces small recurring good-habit nudges (e.g., "run the dishwasher's hot cycle") with adopt/complete/snooze/skip tracking.
- **User flow (Status Board):** Homeowner opens `/dashboard/properties/[id]/status-board`, sees a card grid of home items with status; can "Recompute board" to refresh scoring, and patch an individual item's status.
- **User flow (Daily Snapshot):** `/dashboard/daily-snapshot` shows today's summary deltas (RISK/other kinds) and a list of micro-actions to complete or dismiss; a household `CONTRIBUTOR` role is required to check in or act on micro-actions.
- **User flow (Habit Coach):** `/dashboard/properties/[id]/tools/home-habit-coach` shows a "spotlight" habit, a full list, and history; homeowner can adopt, complete, snooze, skip, dismiss, or reopen a habit, and set preferences for how often to be nudged.
- **Frontend:** `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/properties/[id]/status-board/StatusBoardClient.tsx` + `status-board/components/*` (HeroCard, CardGrid, StatusCard, KPIPills); `.../dashboard/daily-snapshot/page.tsx` + `lib/api/dailySnapshotApi.ts`; `.../properties/[id]/tools/home-habit-coach/HomeHabitCoachClient.tsx`.
- **Backend:** `homeStatusBoard.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/status-board`, `POST .../status-board/recompute`, `PATCH .../status-board/:homeItemId`. `dailyHomePulse.routes.ts`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/daily-snapshot`, `POST .../daily-snapshot/checkin`, `POST .../micro-actions/:actionId/complete|dismiss` (all require `requireHouseholdRole('CONTRIBUTOR')` except the initial GET). `homeHabitCoach.routes.ts`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/home-habits(/spotlight|/history|/preferences|/:habitId)`, `POST .../home-habits/generate`, `POST .../home-habits/:habitId/adopt|complete|snooze|skip|dismiss|reopen|viewed`, `PATCH .../home-habits/preferences`. Controllers: `homeStatusBoard.controller.ts`, `dailyHomePulse.controller.ts`, `homeHabitCoach.controller.ts`.
- **Data:** Home status/item models feeding the board, daily pulse summary + micro-action records, habit + habit-completion-history models, household role checks via `propertyAuth.middleware`.
- **Notes:** None of these three use Gemini — they read/aggregate existing signals (inventory, maintenance, coverage, risk scores) into a simplified view. Household-role gating (`CONTRIBUTOR`+) on pulse/habit actions means a read-only household member can view but not act — worth checking during QA if that distinction is visible in the UI.

### Home Health Nudge Queue (Inventory Verification, Protection Gap & Home Equity)
- **What it does:** A single rotating "nudge" widget on the main dashboard (and inside `/dashboard/protect`) that queues up small prompts across five domains — verifying an unconfirmed inventory item's details (`ASSET`), a resilience choice, a utility check, an insurance protection-gap warning (`INSURANCE`), and a home-equity snapshot prompt (`EQUITY`) — so a homeowner chips away at data completeness a little at a time instead of facing a long form.
- **User flow:** 1) `HomeHealthNudge` widget shows the next highest-priority nudge for the property. 2) For an `ASSET` nudge, the homeowner can snap/upload a label photo (OCR-assisted) or edit the item's fields directly, then confirm — marking the inventory item verified. 3) For an `INSURANCE` nudge, the homeowner can upload a photo of their policy declarations page; the backend OCR-extracts the personal-property limit and deductible for the homeowner to confirm before it's saved to the policy. 4) For an `EQUITY` nudge, the homeowner enters purchase price/date to seed a home-equity estimate. 5) Any nudge can be snoozed or dismissed, which excludes it from the next fetch. 6) A standalone `EquityOverviewCard` reads the same home-equity summary elsewhere on the dashboard.
- **Frontend:** `apps/frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/components/verification/HomeHealthNudge.tsx` (+ `verificationApi.ts`, `InsuranceGapNudge.tsx`, `HomeEquityNudge.tsx`, `useSnoozeManager.ts`), `components/EquityOverviewCard.tsx`, `components/InsuranceSummaryCard.tsx`; also surfaced inside `dashboard/protect/RiskProtectionClient.tsx`.
- **Backend:** `inventoryVerification.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/nudges/next` (cross-domain nudge queue, `getNextDashboardNudge` → `discovery.service.ts`), `GET .../inventory/verification/nudge` (asset-only nudge), `POST .../inventory/:itemId/verify`, `GET .../inventory/verification/stats`, `GET .../insurance/protection-gap` (→ `insuranceAuditor.controller.ts` → `insuranceAuditor.service.ts`), `GET .../value/home-equity` (→ `valueIntelligence.controller.ts` → `valueIntelligence.service.ts`, resolves through the shared financial-context envelope), `POST .../insurance/:policyId/ocr/extract` and `.../ocr/confirm` (→ `insuranceOcr.controller.ts` → `insuranceOcr.service.ts`).
- **Data:** `InventoryItem` (verification fields), `InsurancePolicy` (updated by OCR confirm), plus whatever backs the protection-gap and home-equity calculations (inventory value totals, coverage limits, purchase price/date).
- **Notes:** This is a genuinely cross-cutting feature bundled into one route file by nudge-delivery mechanism rather than by domain — the protection-gap and home-equity pieces are conceptually part of **[Coverage, Risk & Financial Tools](04-coverage-risk-and-financial-tools.md)**, but they're only reachable today through this nudge widget (no dedicated protection-gap or home-equity page was found). **Correction:** insurance-declaration OCR extraction is **not** Gemini — `insuranceOcr.service.ts` (~line 56) preprocesses the image with `sharp`, then dynamically imports and runs **Tesseract.js** (`await import('tesseract.js')`) for local text recognition, the same OCR engine used elsewhere in this cluster (inventory label OCR). A prior version of this line called it "a real Gemini/vision call," which was wrong; a separate page in this wiki also mischaracterized the same file as doing no text recognition at all — see [Coverage, Risk & Financial Tools](04-coverage-risk-and-financial-tools.md#negotiation-shield) for that correction. The asset-verification and nudge-selection logic itself is deterministic. Confirming an OCR extraction also awards a gamification streak (`gamification.service.ts`).

### Documents, Home Records & the Seller's Vault
- **What it does:** Three overlapping-but-distinct places homeowners store paperwork: a simple AI "Magic Scan" Documents feature (upload → Gemini extracts type/fields → stored with soft-delete/trash); a richer Home Records system (typed records with versions, sensitivity/visibility, retention/legal-hold, links to other entities, saved searches, extraction-and-promote-to-Warranty/Expense/InsurancePolicy); and the Seller's Vault, a password- or share-link-protected, unauthenticated-access document bundle for sharing with prospective buyers.
- **User flow (Documents):** `/dashboard/documents` (**correction:** redirects to `/dashboard/vault?tab=documents`, not directly to a property-scoped Documents page as previously stated — see intro) lets a user upload a photo/PDF; Gemini extracts document type and fields (subject to a monthly "Magic Scan" subscription limit); the file goes to S3 and a `Document` row is created with AI-suggested type; documents can be trashed/restored; `/asset-suggestions` fuzzy-matches an uploaded document's extracted brand/model/serial against existing inventory items. Also lists warranties and insurance policies for dropdown use elsewhere.
- **User flow (Home Records):** `/dashboard/properties/[id]/tools/home-records` — upload single or batch files with a record type/sensitivity/visibility, optionally with a retention date or legal hold; add new versions to an existing record over time; link a record to a home event, inventory item, material spec, project, etc.; run AI extraction on a version and review/confirm/correct/reject candidate fields, then promote confirmed data straight into a Warranty, Expense, or Insurance Policy record; save searches; check "storage health" and possible-duplicate-version warnings before upload.
- **User flow (Vault):** Homeowner sets a vault password, optionally generates a short-lived share link; a prospective buyer visits `/vault/:propertyId` and enters the password or uses the signed link (no login required) to view the seller's document bundle. Password attempts are rate-limited (3/hour), share-link views (30/hour). **Correction — the homeowner-side entry point described here is stale:** `/dashboard/vault` no longer leads to a vault-password-setup page; it redirects (`JobHubRedirectPage`) to `/dashboard/properties/:id/vault`, which **itself now redirects again** — to that property's Inventory page with a tab param (confirmed via its own in-code comment, "Vault management page - redirects to inventory with appropriate tab"). Where the owner-side password/share-link setup UI currently lives was not confirmed in this pass; the backend API below (`vault.routes.ts`) is unaffected by any of this frontend churn and is still accurate.
- **Frontend:** Documents: `.../dashboard/documents/DocumentsPageClient.tsx` (property-scoped page). Home Records: `.../properties/[id]/tools/home-records/HomeRecordsClient.tsx`. Vault: `components/vault/VaultView.tsx` is confirmed still rendered, but only at the public buyer-facing `apps/frontend/src/app/vault/[propertyId]/page.tsx` — **not** at `.../properties/[id]/vault/page.tsx`, which is now itself a redirect (see User flow correction above). `warranties/page.tsx` exists but `/dashboard/warranties` now redirects to `/dashboard/vault?tab=coverage` unless an `?action=` query param is present (`next.config.js`) — so it's reachable, but not as a plain standalone page the way this line previously implied.
- **Backend:** `document.routes.ts` mounted at `/api/documents`: `POST /analyze`, `GET /`, `GET /warranties`, `GET /insurance-policies`, `GET /:id/asset-suggestions`, `DELETE /:id` (soft delete), `POST /:id/restore`, `GET /trash`. `homeRecords.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`: full CRUD+lifecycle under `/properties/:propertyId/records` — list/get, `possible-version` check, saved searches, storage-health, create (single + `/batch`), versions (`/versions`, `/versions/:versionId/register-download`, `/download-history`), links (`/links`, `/links/:linkId`), `/archive`, `/trash`, `/restore`, `PATCH /retention`, `PATCH /effective-period`, and extraction (`/versions/:versionId/extract`, `/extractions/:candidateId/review`, `/extractions/promote-warranty|promote-expense|promote-insurance-policy`). `vault.routes.ts` mounted at `/api/vault`: `POST /access/:propertyId` (no auth — password or share token), `GET /status/:propertyId`, `POST /setup/:propertyId` (requires household `OWNER` role), `POST /share-link/:propertyId` (`OWNER` role). Services: `documentIntelligence.service.ts` (Gemini), `homeRecords.service.ts`, `homeRecordsExtraction.service.ts` (Gemini), `vault.service.ts`.
- **Data:** `Document` (soft-delete via `deletedAt`), `Warranty`, `InsurancePolicy`, property record model(s) with versions/links/saved-searches, vault password hash + share tokens on `Property`.
- **Notes:** Documents/analyze and Home Records extraction both use **Gemini** via `documentIntelligence.service.ts` (`gemini-2.0-flash`), which throws at construction if `GEMINI_API_KEY` is unset — callers that need to tolerate a missing key (e.g. `materialSpec.service.ts`'s photo extraction) deliberately lazy-`require()` it instead of importing at module top level. Vault access is intentionally unauthenticated by design (buyers have no account) — access is rate-limited per property/IP instead. Document deletion throughout is soft-delete (trash + restore), explicitly to avoid breaking evidence links other domains (claims, insurance, warranties, home events) hold on a document.

### Energy Auditor & Material Specs
- **What it does:** Energy Auditor turns utility bill uploads + a short questionnaire into an AI energy-efficiency report with savings recommendations. Material Specs is a catalog of finish/material choices per property (paint colors, flooring, fixtures) with photos, lifecycle state, substitution history, and AI-assisted photo extraction of spec details, plus export and a "repair reorder" lookup (e.g., "what's the exact paint to reorder").
- **User flow (Energy Auditor):** `/dashboard/energy` — homeowner fills in usage/home characteristics (sq ft, occupants, electric heat/AC/pool/solar flags) and optionally uploads up to 3 bill files; submits; backend first checks the property has enough context (size/location) via `getPropertyContext`/`evaluateEnergyApplicability` before generating the Gemini-backed audit.
- **User flow (Material Specs):** `/dashboard/properties/[id]/materials` — browse/search specs, create a new spec, add photos (which trigger fire-and-forget AI field extraction), review/confirm extracted fields, transition a spec's lifecycle or record a substitution, and request an export of the whole materials list.
- **Frontend:** `.../dashboard/energy/page.tsx` + `components/EnergyAuditor.tsx`. `.../properties/[id]/materials/MaterialSpecsClient.tsx`, `.../materials/[specId]/SpecDetailClient.tsx`, `materialSpecApi.ts`, `materialSpecLaunchContext.ts`.
- **Backend:** `energyAuditor.routes.ts` mounted at `/api/energy`: `POST /audit`. `materialSpec.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/materials(/search)`, `GET/POST .../materials`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE .../materials/:specId`, `POST .../:specId/lifecycle|substitutions|extractions`, `POST .../extractions/:reviewId/review`, `GET .../:specId/repair-reorder`, photos (`POST/DELETE .../photos`, `POST .../photos/upload`, `PATCH .../photos/reorder`), exports (`GET/POST .../materials/exports`, `GET .../exports/:exportId`). Services: `energyAuditor.service.ts` (Gemini), `materialSpec.service.ts` (lazy-loads `documentIntelligenceService` for photo extraction).
- **Data:** Energy audit report/recommendation model(s), `MaterialSpec` + photos + `MaterialExtractionReview` + substitution/lifecycle history, export job model.
- **Notes:** Energy Auditor is gated on property completeness — it returns HTTP 409 with an `applicability` payload rather than generating a report if size/location data is missing, which is a real, deliberate guard (not a bug) worth knowing about when demoing. Both Energy Auditor and Material Spec photo extraction are **Gemini**-backed (`gemini-2.0-flash`); material photo extraction never blocks or fails the upload itself — a failed extraction just means no review row is created.

### Plant Advisor (Room Plant Recommendations)
- **What it does:** A lighter, non-AI monetization-adjacent feature: recommends houseplants per room based on a light/goal/pet-safety profile the homeowner fills in, tracks plants actually added to the home and a basic care outlook/reminders, and supports outdoor "garden zones."
- **User flow:** `/dashboard/properties/[id]/tools/plant-advisor` — set a room's plant profile (light level, maintenance tolerance, pet safety, goal), generate recommendations from a static catalog, save or dismiss a recommendation, add it to the home as a tracked plant, and view a care outlook across all tracked plants; garden zones are managed similarly for outdoor spaces.
- **Frontend:** `.../properties/[id]/tools/plant-advisor/PlantAdvisorClient.tsx`, `components/PlantAdvisorDashboardCard.tsx`.
- **Backend:** `roomPlantAdvisor.routes.ts` mounted at `/api`: `GET /properties/:propertyId/plant-advisor/care-outlook`, plants (`POST /plants`, `PATCH /plants/:plantId/care`), garden zones (`POST/PATCH /garden-zones(/:zoneId)`), rooms (`GET /rooms`, `GET /rooms/:roomId`), catalog (`GET /catalog`), profile (`PUT /rooms/:roomId/profile`), recommendations (`POST /rooms/:roomId/recommendations/generate`, `POST .../:recommendationId/save|dismiss|add-to-home`). Service: `roomPlantAdvisor.service.ts`.
- **Data:** `PlantCatalog`, room plant profile/recommendation models, tracked home-plant + garden-zone models, `RoomType`/`PlantGoalType`/`PlantLightLevel`/`PlantMaintenanceLevel`/`PlantToxicityLevel` enums.
- **Notes:** Confirmed **not** Gemini-backed — recommendations come from filtering a static `PlantCatalog` against the room profile, not a model call. Per prior product direction this feature is meant to stay in nav permanently with a planned plant-seller commerce tie-in, not to be treated as dead weight.
