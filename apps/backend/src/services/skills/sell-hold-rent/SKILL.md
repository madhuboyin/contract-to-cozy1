# Sell, Hold, or Rent Skill

## Purpose

Compare governed sell, hold, and rent scenarios from recorded property and financial context.

## Select this Skill when

- Should I sell, hold, or rent this home?
- Compare selling versus renting out my property
- Would I be better off holding or selling?
- Should I sell or rent this propertie?

## Do not select this Skill when

- Guarantee future rent, appreciation, or sale proceeds

## Operations

- `SELL_HOLD_RENT_ANALYSIS`

## Consumers

- ASK: SELL_HOLD_RENT_ANALYSIS
