# Seller Preparation Skill

## Purpose

Navigate seller readiness and major home-sale preparation using recorded home context.

## Select this Skill when

- Help me prepare for selling my home
- Give me a checklist for my home sale
- What should I do before moving out?
- Help me prepare for my home sale and seling

## Do not select this Skill when

- Guarantee an exact sale price or legal disclosure compliance

## Operations

- `MAJOR_EVENT_ENTRY`

## Consumers

- ASK: MAJOR_EVENT_ENTRY
