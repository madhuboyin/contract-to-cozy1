# Break-Even Skill

## Purpose

Show when owning this home breaks even, as projected appreciation catches up with cumulative ownership costs, with a conservative-to-optimistic range.

## Select this Skill when

- Show my home break-even analysis
- When will my home break even?
- Has owning this house paid off yet?

## Do not select this Skill when

- Guarantee my home will be worth more than I paid when I sell
- The request is about a refinance break-even (`refinance`), or the payback of an upgrade, renovation or replacement
- The request is to compare selling, holding or renting (`sell-hold-rent`) or to list current ownership costs (`ownership-cost`)

## Operations

- `BREAK_EVEN_ANALYSIS`

## Consumers

- ASK: BREAK_EVEN_ANALYSIS

## Canonical ownership and boundaries

Operations remain owned by their registered canonical services and may be reached only through the adapters declared in the machine manifest. Context access is limited to declared providers. Peer Skill execution is prohibited; handoffs return to Ask for normal routing and authorization.
