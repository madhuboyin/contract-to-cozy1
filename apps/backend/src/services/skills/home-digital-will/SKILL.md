# Home Continuity Plan Skill

## Purpose

Create and manage this home's continuity plan inside Ask Cozy: how ready it is to hand off, what each section holds and who the trusted contacts are. Durable answers remain privacy-minimized; sensitive details appear only in their governed input and review workspace.

## Select this Skill when

- Show my home continuity plan
- Is our house handoff plan ready if someone has to take over?
- Who are the trusted contacts on my home plan?

## Do not select this Skill when

- Make my home plan legally binding as my will
- The request is about a legal will, estate or inheritance
- The request is about household members and roles or the home record (`property-record`)

## Operations

- `HOME_DIGITAL_WILL`
- `HOME_DIGITAL_WILL_CREATE`

## Consumers

- ASK: HOME_DIGITAL_WILL, HOME_DIGITAL_WILL_CREATE

## Canonical ownership and boundaries

Operations remain owned by their registered canonical services and may be reached only through the adapters declared in the machine manifest. Context access is limited to declared providers. Peer Skill execution is prohibited; handoffs return to Ask for normal routing and authorization.
