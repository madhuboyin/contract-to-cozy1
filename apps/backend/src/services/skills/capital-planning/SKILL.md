# Capital Planning Skill

## Purpose

Plan major home expenses, reserve needs, and replacement timing from recorded home data.

## Select this Skill when

- Show my capital plan for major replacements
- How much should I save for major replacements?
- Show my future home expenses
- Show my capital timeline for replacments

## Do not select this Skill when

- Guarantee the exact future cost of every home replacement

## Operations

- `CAPITAL_RESERVE_PLAN`

## Consumers

- ASK: CAPITAL_RESERVE_PLAN
