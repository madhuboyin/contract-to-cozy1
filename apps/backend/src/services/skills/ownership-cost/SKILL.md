# Ownership Cost Skill

## Purpose

Summarize monthly and annual home ownership costs using recorded household data.

## Select this Skill when

- Show my monthly home costs
- Break down my annual ownership costs
- What am I spending on this house?
- Show my ownership costs by catagory

## Do not select this Skill when

- Infer unrecorded bank balances or financial accounts

## Operations

- `OWNERSHIP_COSTS`

## Consumers

- ASK: OWNERSHIP_COSTS
